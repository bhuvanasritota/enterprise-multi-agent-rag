EnterpriseRAG Demo Employee Handbook 2026

Annual Leave: Full-time employees receive 18 paid annual leave days per calendar year. Requests of five consecutive working days or more require manager approval at least 14 calendar days in advance.

Sick Leave: Full-time employees receive 10 paid sick leave days per calendar year. A medical certificate may be requested after three consecutive sick days.

Approval Process: Employees submit leave in the HR portal. The direct manager reviews the request. Requests longer than 10 working days also require HR approval.
